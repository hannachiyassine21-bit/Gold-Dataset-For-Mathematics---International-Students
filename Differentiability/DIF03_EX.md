### Exercise DIF03 (Hard)

Let $f(x) = \ln(1 + e^{-x})$ evaluated on the domain $[0, +\infty[$.

**Questions:**
1. Show that for all $x \in [0, +\infty[$, $|f'(x)| \le \frac{1}{2}$.
2. Prove that for all $a, b \in [0, +\infty[$ where $a < b$, we have:
$$|\ln(1+e^{-b}) - \ln(1+e^{-a})| \le \frac{1}{2}|b - a|$$
