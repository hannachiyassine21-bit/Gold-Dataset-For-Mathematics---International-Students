### Correction DIF02

We compute the limit of the rate of change at $0$:
$$\lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} = \lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{\sqrt{x}\cos(x) - 0}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{\cos(x)}{\sqrt{x}}$$

Since $\lim_{x \to 0^+} \cos(x) = 1$ and $\lim_{x \to 0^+} \sqrt{x} = 0^+$, we find:
$$\lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} = +\infty$$

**Conclusion:** The function $f$ is not differentiable at $x = 0$ from the right. Geometrically, the representative curve $\zeta_f$ admits a vertical half-tangent directed upwards at the origin point $(0,0)$.
