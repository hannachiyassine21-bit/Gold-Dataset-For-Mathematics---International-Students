### Correction LIM01

1. Direct substitution yields $\frac{0}{0}$. Factor the numerator polynomial to eliminate the structural zero factor:
$$\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x-2} = \lim_{x \to 2} (x + 2) = 2 + 2 = 4$$

2. For the limit at infinity of a rational function, isolate the dominant highest power terms:
$$\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x^2(2 - \frac{3}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2})}{x^2(5 + \frac{7}{x^2})} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{2 - 0 + 0}{5 + 0} = \frac{2}{5}$$
