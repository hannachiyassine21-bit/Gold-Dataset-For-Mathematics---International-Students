### Exercise LIM09 (Hard)

Let $f$ be a piecewise function defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by:
$$f(x) = \begin{cases} 
      x^2 + a x + b & \text{if } x \le 1 \\
      \ln(x) & \text{if } x > 1 
   \end{cases}$$

**Question:** Find the values of the real parameters $a$ and $b$ such that $f$ is continuous and differentiable at $x = 1$.
