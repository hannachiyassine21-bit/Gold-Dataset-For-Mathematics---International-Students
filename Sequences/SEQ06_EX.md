### Exercise SEQ06 (Hard)

Consider the sequence $(I_n)$ defined for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ by:
$$I_n = \int_0^1 \frac{x^n}{1 + x^2} dx$$

**Questions:**
1. Show that $(I_n)$ is strictly decreasing and bounded below.
2. Prove that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $0 \le I_n \le \frac{1}{n+1}$, and deduce $\lim_{n \to \infty} I_n$.
