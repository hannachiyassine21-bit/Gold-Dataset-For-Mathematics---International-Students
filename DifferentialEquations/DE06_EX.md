### Exercise DE06 (Hard)

Consider the differential equation with variable coefficients for $x > 0$:
$$(E): x^2 y'' + 3xy' + y = 0$$

**Question:** By changing variables using the substitution link $x = e^t$ (which implies $t = \ln(x)$), transform $(E)$ into a constant coefficient second-order equation, and find its general solution expression.
