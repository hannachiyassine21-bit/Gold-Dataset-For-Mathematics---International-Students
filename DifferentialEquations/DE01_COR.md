### Correction DE01

The differential equation is of the baseline homogeneous linear structure $y' = ay$ where $a = 3$.
The general solutions on $\mathbb{R}$ are expressed by:
$$y(x) = C \cdot e^{3x} \quad (\text{where } C \in \mathbb{R})$$

We use the initial boundary coordinate condition to isolate the unique tracking solution:
$$y(0) = 4 \iff C \cdot e^{3(0)} = 4 \iff C \cdot 1 = 4 \iff C = 4$$

**Conclusion:** The unique valid solution is $y(x) = 4e^{3x}$.
